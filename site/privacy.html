PhoneSaar Open web app

Privacy

Everything runs on your device and nothing is uploaded. PhoneSaar has no account, no server of its own, no analytics and no ads.

The web app

It talks to your phone directly over the USB cable, from inside your browser tab. What it reads from the phone (app names, memory, battery) stays in that tab. The list of changes you made, used for Undo, is kept in your browser's local storage on this computer. Clear your site data to remove it.

The Android app

It connects to your own phone, or to another phone or TV on your network. Its undo log and paired devices stay in the app's private storage.

The one network request

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, and the Android app is downloaded from GitHub, so GitHub sees those requests like any website. PhoneSaar sends nothing about you or your phone anywhere.
